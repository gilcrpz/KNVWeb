About
Kusina ni Venus

Founded in 2017 as a cozy kubo-style eatery, Kusina ni Venus brings you the rich, nostalgic flavors of classic lutong bahay. From our signature Lomi Batangas to timeless home-cooked favorites, every dish is crafted to share with family and friends.
